Looks — every outfit ({{ person.outfits|length }})

A look is a run of frames in the same clothes, found from the body crop itself: the torso band in Lab colour. On this hub's own archive two frames two minutes apart differ by 0.11 median and two frames of another day by 0.47, so the bar sits at 0.30 and a look starts only after two frames in a row disagree — a day with more than one look means {{ person.name }} changed clothes. The newest usable frame of a look is what generation is handed as a reference (F-209).
